## What is Lunala?

Lunala starts Rocket League for you and adds things the game doesn't have: map swaps, an in-game menu, Freeplay training tools, a live camera and more. It's free.

## Features

- **Freeplay Swap**: play workshop maps in any Soccar arena.
- **Map Merge**: any arena with another arena's scenery, sky and colours (with Lunala+ also with Anti-Cheat).
- **Lunala Menu in game**: items (painted versions too), presets, custom decals and balls, and tweaks like game speed and unlimited flips.
- **Freeplay training**: Setups on any button, Freeplay Checkpoints (save, jump back, freeze and rewind), Limit Boost in Setups and a ball speed display.
- **Live camera**: exact camera values, changed in the launcher while you play.
- **Freeplay replays**: save your Freeplay session as a normal Rocket League replay.
- **Boost**: more performance while you play.
- **Discord Rich Presence**: shows what you're doing in Rocket League.
- **Themes**: pick a colour, and Lunala follows it.

## Lunala+

Lunala+ is a one-time purchase that adds features that also work **with Anti-Cheat**, so you can use them online:

- **Item Swap**: make an item you own look like any other item, in any paint.
- **Extended Camera Settings**: exact camera values, also with Anti-Cheat.
- **Custom Decals**: your own decal images on your car.
- **Custom Colors**: any colour for your car.
- **Custom Ball Skins**: your own image on the ball.
- **Reset Indicator Color**: any colour for the flip reset indicator.
- **Custom Crossbar Sounds**: your own sound when the ball hits the crossbar.

Get it in Lunala: open **Lunala+** and click **Get Lunala+**. After paying you get a key on the page and by email. Click **Redeem key** in Lunala and paste it. Each key works once.

Everything Lunala+ changes is only visible to you.

## Download and install

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and Rocket League on Steam or Epic Games.

1. Download `Lunala.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/zusmori/Lunala/releases/latest).
2. Start it. Pick Steam or Epic Games. Lunala finds your game and updates itself.
3. Press **Play** and choose with or without Anti-Cheat.
4. Without Anti-Cheat, press **F2** in game to open the Lunala Menu (you can change the key in Settings).

## Antivirus warnings

Some antivirus programs flag Lunala by mistake. Lunala is never packed or hidden, and it never changes your antivirus. I'm working on getting these false positives removed.

## Good to know

Lunala is an unofficial fan project and not affiliated with Psyonix or Epic Games. The Lunala Menu, items and map swaps only work without Anti-Cheat, which means offline modes only. Use at your own risk.
